Quick Guide

TfL's service updates can sometimes feel like a puzzle. This tool cuts through the noise and shows you exactly what's happening on your route using simple, interactive line diagrams.

🗺️ Network Map

Your main dashboard. It breaks down London's transport network into clear groups—Underground & Rail lines and London Overground lines. Tap on any line to see a visual map that forks at real junctions.

🔴 Live Status

A quick-glance list of every line, automatically sorted so any line with delays or closures pops to the top. Interchange badges show connecting lines at major stations.

🚧 Upcoming Works

Provides advance warning of planned engineering work scheduled up to 7 days in advance, so weekend closures don't catch you by surprise.
